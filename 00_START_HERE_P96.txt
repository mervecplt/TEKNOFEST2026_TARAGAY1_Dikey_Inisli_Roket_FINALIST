P96 ACTIVE-BRAKE 100-ADC CLOSED-LOOP OPEN TEST - BENCH ONLY

Purpose:
- Repeat the replacement-pot 100-ADC OPEN test, but stop motor inertia with dynamic braking.
- Verify that the actuator can settle inside a real +/-8 ADC target band instead of coasting past it.

Automatic sequence, once per MCU reset:
1) Wait 5 seconds.
2) Require P83 feedback_valid=1 and mode=NORMAL.
3) Capture START = p83_filtered_adc.
4) TARGET = START - 100 ADC.
5) Drive OPEN using 50.2% PWM short chunks.
6) At every pulse end, PWM goes to 0/0 but BOTH bridge enable pins stay asserted.
7) Hold dynamic brake for 100 ms.
8) Re-check filtered ADC. If still above target band, issue the next bounded chunk.
9) Success only after 5 consecutive fresh P83 samples remain inside TARGET +/-8 ADC.
10) After success or fault, bridge is fully disabled.

Adaptive chunk durations:
- remaining >55 ADC: 20 ms
- remaining >28 ADC: 12 ms
- remaining >14 ADC: 8 ms
- otherwise: 5 ms
PWM remains 128/255 (~50.2%) to preserve breakaway torque.

Hard limits:
- max 8 chunks
- max 100 ms cumulative motor-on time
- max 3.0 s total sequence time
- invalid feedback, wrong direction, or settled position below TARGET-8 aborts
- one automatic sequence per reset

Important P96 telemetry behavior:
- needle_enabled=1 with LPWM=0 and RPWM=0 during the dynamic-brake hold.
- needle_enabled=0 only after success/fault when the bridge is fully inhibited.

RCS forced safe. NRF/UART RX command restrictions inherited from diagnostic profile.
NO GAS. NO PRESSURE. Keep hands/tools clear of motor, gearbox, coupling and valve.
