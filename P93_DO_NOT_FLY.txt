Startup torque boost:
- First OPEN pulse: 128/255 PWM = ~50.2% for 40 ms.
- If feedback responds and remains valid: later OPEN pulses are 60/255 = ~23.5% for 20 ms.
- 30 ms settle between pulses.
- If start is 1023 and filtered ADC is still exactly 1023 after the boosted pulse + settle, sequence aborts before a second pulse.
- Existing total motor-on and sequence time limits remain active.

The boost raises average motor voltage/current during breakaway, but does not prove mechanical torque or repair the PC1/pot feedback path.
