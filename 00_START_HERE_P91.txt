P91 - 400 ADC CLOSED-LOOP BENCH JOG (DO NOT FLY)

NO GAS / NO PRESSURE. RCS POWER OFF.
Hands/tools clear of motor, gearbox, coupling and valve shaft.
Keep a physical motor-power cutoff available.

WHAT IT DOES
- Waits 5 s after diagnostic service init.
- Requires P83 feedback VALID + NORMAL.
- Requires starting filtered ADC 450..900.
- OPEN direction target = start ADC - 400 ADC.
- Drives in 20 ms chunks at PWM 60/255 (~23.5%).
- Waits 30 ms between chunks and re-checks feedback.
- Stops within +5 ADC of target.

HARD STOPS
- feedback invalid / not NORMAL
- >20 ADC wrong-direction movement
- 400 ms cumulative motor-on time
- 2500 ms total sequence time
- one sequence maximum per reset

IMPORTANT
If ADC is stuck at 1023, P91 WILL NOT MOVE. That is intentional. Exact
400-ADC motion is impossible to control safely until feedback is measurable.

UART
Use monitor_uart_p87_adc_sweep.py or monitor_uart_p91_400adc.py.
Recommended:
  python -u monitor_uart_p91_400adc.py --port COM21 --log uart_p91_400adc.txt
