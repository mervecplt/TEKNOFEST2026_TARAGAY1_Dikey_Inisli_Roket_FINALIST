1) WHY P82
---------
P81 suppressed normal noise well, but a multi-sample common-mode event could pass
its 3-sample (~6 ms) persistence check and create a large filtered jump. P82 adds
physical-rate limits, direction-consistent persistence, output slew limiting, and
post-glitch quarantine.
