P50 validation summary
======================

Basis: P49R1 bench evidence (365.035 s SD log, 3000 UART frames).
Observed deterministic timing relationship:
  zupt_joseph_updates = 4020
  miss_imu             = 1340
  4020 / 3             = 1340
Therefore every 3-axis stationary ZUPT event caused one 1 kHz IMU release miss.

P50 change:
- Exact P49 masked Joseph semantics retained.
- BGX/BGY/BGZ gains remain zero.
- BGxBG 3x3 covariance block remains untouched.
- Full software-double Joseph expression replaced by its exact rank-one form
  P_ij <- P_ij - (P_ik/S)*P_jk for retained-gain rows.
- Hardware float is used on Cortex-M4F.
- Existing covariance clamp, integrity guard and state-preserving rollback remain.
