- Based on P92.

- Subsequent pulses remain 60/255 for 20 ms.
- Upper-rail no-response abort remains after the first boosted pulse.
- RCS safe, NRF/UART RX diagnostic restrictions inherited from P92.
- BENCH ONLY, NO GAS / NO PRESSURE.
