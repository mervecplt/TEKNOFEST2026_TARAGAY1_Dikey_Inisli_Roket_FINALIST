TARAGAY-T1 P83 RCS DIRECTION / GPIO OBSERVER
=============================================
Observer-only. Do not use these fields as control inputs.

CubeIDE Live Expressions:

solenoid_status.requested_mask
solenoid_status.applied_mask
solenoid_status.physical_command_mask
solenoid_status.physical_readback_mask
solenoid_status.physical_readback_ok
solenoid_status.direction_map_revision
solenoid_status.physical_write_count
solenoid_status.physical_readback_mismatch_count
solenoid_status.last_request_us
solenoid_status.last_physical_write_us
solenoid_status.last_request_to_write_us
solenoid_status.max_request_to_write_us

Expected 2026-09-09 swapped mapping (physical mask bits = IN1,IN2,IN3,IN4):
logical X+ 0x01 -> physical IN2 0x02
logical X- 0x02 -> physical IN1 0x01
logical Y+ 0x04 -> physical IN4 0x08
logical Y- 0x08 -> physical IN3 0x04

physical_readback_ok should remain 1 after each GPIO transition.
physical_readback_mismatch_count should remain 0.

Note: requested_mask and applied_mask deliberately remain in LOGICAL control-sign
convention. Only physical_command_mask/readback_mask are raw relay-channel masks.
