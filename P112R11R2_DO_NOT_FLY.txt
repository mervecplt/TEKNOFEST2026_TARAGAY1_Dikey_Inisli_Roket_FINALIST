P112R11R2 950+ NEEDLE VALVE MECHANICAL COMMISSIONING

DO NOT FLY.
Use only on a depressurized/inert bench with PE9 physically connected.
Main propulsion pressure/gas/propellant/ignition/energetics must remain physically isolated.
