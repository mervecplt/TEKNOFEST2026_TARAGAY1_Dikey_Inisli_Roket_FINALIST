P108 PRE-BRAKE + COAST CHARACTERIZATION - DO NOT FLY

BENCH ONLY. NO GAS. NO PRESSURE.

Purpose:
P107 showed loaded motion with PWM144 boost + PWM128 sustain, but a 50 ADC
command overshot. P108 keeps the same power and characterizes how far the
mechanism continues moving after an intentionally early active-brake command.

Automatic sequence:
- start gate: 850..1023 ADC
- target = start - 50 ADC
- pre-brake threshold = target + 20 = start - 30 ADC
- 20 ms PWM144 OPEN boost
- immediate PWM128 sustain, maximum 60 ms
- active dynamic brake when pre-brake threshold is reached
- record filtered ADC at brake entry, +100 ms, +250 ms, +500 ms
- then bridge completely OFF
- NO automatic CLOSE/return

Hard safety limits:
- PWM never exceeds 144/255 (~56.5%)
- total powered OPEN time <= 80 ms
- hard overshoot / wrong direction / feedback loss aborts
- keep a physical motor-power cutoff immediately available
- if motor hard-stalls, coupler twists/slips, alignment binds or a hard stop is
  contacted, cut motor power immediately and do not repeat
