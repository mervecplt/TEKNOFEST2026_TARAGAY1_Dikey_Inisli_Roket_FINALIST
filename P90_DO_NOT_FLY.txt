TARAGAY-T1 P90 - DO NOT FLY
===========================

BENCH-ONLY MOTOR DIAGNOSTIC.
NO GAS / NO PRESSURE. KEEP RCS POWER OFF.

Behavior:
- No PA0/button approval.
- No P83 feedback-valid/mode/ADC-range approval.
- 5 s fixed delay after diagnostic service initialization.
- Exactly one OPEN pulse per boot.
- PWM = 13/255 = 5.10% duty.
- Pulse = 100 ms.
- TIM7 1 kHz hard cutoff + 110 ms independent fast-service fallback.
- Bridge disabled after pulse.
- P87/P83 ADC data remain telemetry only.

POWER-ON/RESET CAN CAUSE AUTOMATIC MOTOR MOTION.
Keep hands/tools clear of motor, gearbox, coupler and valve shaft.
Have a physical motor-power cutoff available.
