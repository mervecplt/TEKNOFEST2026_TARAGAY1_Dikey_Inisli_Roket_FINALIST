DO NOT FLY / NOT FLIGHT-QUALIFIED.

Use only on a fully depressurized/inert bench. Propellant/gas/pressure,
pyrotechnics/ignition/energetic hardware must be physically disconnected or
isolated. RCS and vent physical loads remain software-hard-isolated in this
profile and should also remain physically isolated for the test.

move if the real GeneratedFlightControl produces a nonzero command. Keep the
mechanical travel clear/restrained and the valve/actuator depressurized.

flight logic.
