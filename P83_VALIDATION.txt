=================================================================
Firmware: 8.19M-P83-BOOT-SAFE-REACQUIRE-FEEDBACK
Purpose : Fix P82 bad-start-anchor / non-recovering quarantine failure

1) ROOT CAUSE ADDRESSED
-----------------------
P82 could initialize filtered position from an early transient. If the real PC1
signal later settled far from that bad anchor, quarantine recovery required the
median to return within +/-25 ADC of the bad last-good and could stay invalid
forever.

P83 removes direct startup initialization. The first anchor requires a compact
median cluster for ~200 ms. Quarantine recovery also tracks a stable cluster
independent of last-good and, in this motor-locked diagnostic image only, can
re-anchor after ~500 ms of stability.
