TARAGAY-T1 P90 - ADC BYPASS + 5% AUTO MOTOR JOG (BENCH ONLY)
============================================================
Firmware: 8.19M-P90-ADC-BYPASS-5PCT-AUTO-JOG

WHAT IT DOES
------------
- Keeps P87/P83 ADC sweep telemetry running.
- Does NOT wait for PA0/button confirmation.
- Does NOT require feedback_valid, P83 NORMAL, or ADC 150..900.
- Five seconds after NeedleValveIntegrationTest initializes, automatically
  arms the bridge and commands OPEN at 13/255 PWM (~5.10%) for 100 ms.
- Exactly one motor pulse per MCU boot/reset.
- TIM7 1 kHz ISR is the hard 100 ms cutoff.
- Fast service independently stops the bridge at ~110 ms if required.
- After the pulse the bridge is disabled and the jog arm is cleared.

IMPORTANT
---------
5.10% is PWM duty, NOT 5% valve position. This image intentionally bypasses
ADC authorization because it is only for isolating the motor/H-bridge/mechanics
from the known PC1 feedback problem.

POWER-ON/RESET CAN CAUSE AUTOMATIC MOTOR MOTION AFTER ~5 s.
NO GAS / NO PRESSURE. RCS POWER OFF. KEEP HANDS/TOOLS CLEAR.
HAVE A PHYSICAL MOTOR-POWER CUTOFF AVAILABLE.
DO NOT FLY THIS IMAGE.

BUILD
-----
STM32CubeIDE: Clean Project -> Build Project -> Flash.

  8.19M-P90-ADC-BYPASS-5PCT-AUTO-JOG
